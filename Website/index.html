Water Quality Analysis

1. pH Analysis

The recorded pH is 7.4, indicating slightly alkaline water. This is generally within a suitable range for many aquaculture systems.

2. Turbidity Analysis

Turbidity is measured at 12 NTU. This indicates relatively low water cloudiness, although suspended particles should continue to be monitored.

3. Dissolved Oxygen Analysis

Dissolved oxygen is 6.8 mg/L. This indicates good oxygen availability for many commonly farmed fish species.

4. Temperature Analysis

The water temperature is 27.5°C. This is suitable for several warm-water aquaculture species, depending on the species being cultivated.

5. Ammonia Analysis

The measured ammonia concentration is 0.02 mg/L. This is a relatively low reading. Regular monitoring is important because ammonia toxicity also depends on pH and temperature.

Recommendations

  • Continue monitoring dissolved oxygen levels regularly.
  • Maintain stable water temperature conditions.
  • Monitor ammonia concentration to prevent harmful accumulation.
  • Check turbidity regularly to identify changes in suspended particles.
  • Maintain appropriate pH conditions for the specific aquatic species.
